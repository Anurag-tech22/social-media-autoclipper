# 🎬 90-Second Hackathon Demo Video Script

Use this script when recording your demo video (Loom, OBS, or YouTube unlisted link) for your Devpost submission.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline Breakdown (Total: 90 seconds)

### 0:00 - 0:15 | The Hook & Problem
- **What to show**: Screen recording of a long 30-minute podcast / YouTube video.
- **What to say**:
  > *"Every content creator knows that short-form video on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts is where reach explodes. But manually turning a 30-minute podcast into vertical clips takes over 2 hours of scrubbing timelines, cropping to 9:16, and timing subtitles. Today, we built **Auto-Clip & Burn AI** to solve this in under 60 seconds."*

---

### 0:15 - 0:45 | Live Demonstration (Capturing the Bonus Points!)
- **What to show**: Open `http://localhost:8501`, paste a YouTube URL (or click "Load Synthetic Demo Clip"), select the **🔥 Hormozi Punch** preset, and click **"⚡ Generate Viral Vertical Shorts"**.
- **What to say**:
  > *"Watch this live: I paste a video link into Auto-Clip & Burn. I select my style preset—like the Alex Hormozi bold yellow theme—and hit Generate.*
  >
  > *In real-time, the app extracts the audio stream, runs OpenAI Whisper with word-level microsecond alignment, and uses our speech-density algorithm to identify the top 3 most engaging, high-energy moments.*
  >
  > *Then, it converts the 16:9 widescreen footage into centered 1080x1920 vertical shorts, adds an AI viral hook headline at the top, burns an animated retention progress bar at the bottom, and renders word-by-word highlighted captions."*

---

### 0:45 - 1:15 | The Results & Features
- **What to show**: Scroll to the **Generated 9:16 Vertical Shorts** section. Hit play on Short #1. Show the animated captions in sync with speech and click the **Download MP4** button.
- **What to say**:
  > *"Look at the final result: We get three perfectly formatted 9:16 vertical Shorts. The caption words highlight dynamically as the speaker talks, the top hook grabs attention immediately, and the bottom progress bar maximizes viewer retention.*
  >
  > *And with one single click, I can download the high-definition MP4 file ready to upload to YouTube Shorts or Instagram."*

---

### 1:15 - 1:30 | Technical Wrap-up & Impact
- **What to show**: Quick flash of the GitHub repo and Streamlit interface.
- **What to say**:
  > *"Best of all, Auto-Clip & Burn AI is 100% open-source, runs on free-tier infrastructure with zero API costs, and saves creators 10+ hours every single week. Thank you for watching!"*
