🎯 1. What is This Project & Why Was It Made?

Short-form video is the #1 organic audience growth channel. However, editors spend over 2 hours per video scrubbing timelines, keyframing subtitle colors, and manually cropping frames. Commercial SaaS tools charge $50–$80/month for basic auto-clipping.

Auto-Clip & Burn AI completely automates the workflow with zero subscription fees, providing frame-accurate Whisper AI transcription, engagement scoring, luminous neon karaoke captions, viral top hook banners, and dynamic progress bars.

❌ The Traditional Problem
  • 2–3 hours manual timeline editing
  • High recurring software costs
  • Manual word-by-word subtitle styling
  • Center cropping cuts off code & slides
✅ The Auto-Clip Solution
  • 45-second 1-click batch generation
  • 100% Free & Open-Source pipeline
  • Automated glowing karaoke typography
  • Fit Canvas mode preserves 100% full screen

🛠️ 4. Technology Stack

Frontend & Studio Layer

Streamlit framework with custom dark glassmorphic CSS, real-time sidebar caption preview, and multi-tab input handling.

AI Speech Intelligence

OpenAI Whisper AI engine with greedy fast-path decoding, English optimization, and word-level alignment.

Video Engineering Engine

FFmpeg 7.x multi-threaded pipeline with Lanczos scaling, 36x downscaled boxblur canvas, and ASS subtitle burning.

DevOps & Deployment

Fully configured for Streamlit Community Cloud via packages.txt (FFmpeg) and pinned requirements.txt.
